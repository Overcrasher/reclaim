Reclaim

A calm pause before you scroll

Reclaim is a free Chrome extension that adds a calm pause before the sites that swallow your time. Open a popular social or video site and a quiet screen asks what you are here for. Take a few seconds, then decide: go in for a set time, or leave.

Coming soon to the Chrome Web Store

Free, no ads, no accounts. Works in Chrome.

How it works

  1. A calm pauseA full-screen pause appears before the page is usable, with a short countdown. Playing media is paused until you choose. Write what you are here for if you like.
  2. Go in for a set time, or leave"Go in" starts a timed session (10 minutes by default). When it ends, a soft reminder offers a few more minutes or a way out. "Leave" takes you to a small page that says how much time you reclaimed.
  3. Watch your plant growThe popup shows today's time, a 7-day chart and a weekly summary, with a little plant that grows as your weeks go well. No guilt text, just visuals.

Your sites, your rules

Reclaim comes with a set of popular social and video sites, and you can add or remove sites in its settings. For a site you add, Chrome asks your permission first. "Time reclaimed" is an estimate, and it is labelled that way everywhere. Reclaim is an independent project and is not affiliated with any site.

Private by design

Everything stays on your device. The things you type as intentions are deleted automatically after 7 days. There are no ads, no tracking and no accounts, and the extension makes no network requests. It is open source, so you can check for yourself. Read the full privacy policy.

Install

Reclaim will be available from the Chrome Web Store. Developers can already load the source unpacked from GitHub.

Support

Reclaim is free. If it helps you, you can optionally make a one-time contribution through Stripe. The option appears only at milestones of reclaimed hours, and you can turn it off at any time.
